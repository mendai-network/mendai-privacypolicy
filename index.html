Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Mendai collects, uses, stores, processes, protects, and shares information when you access or use:

  • the Mendai mobile application;
  • AI Guide;
  • AI-powered matching services;
  • messaging features;
  • profile features;
  • future Mendai products and services;
  • websites operated by Mendai; and
  • any related software, APIs, and digital services (collectively, the “Service”).

By creating an account or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Service.
