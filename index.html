MENDAI PRIVACY POLICY

Part 1 – Introduction, Scope, Information We Collect, AI Processing, and Account Information

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mendai (“Mendai,” “we,” “our,” or “us”).

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Mendai collects, uses, stores, processes, protects, and shares information when you access or use:

  • the Mendai mobile application;
  • AI Guide;
  • AI-powered matching services;
  • messaging features;
  • profile features;
  • future Mendai products and services;
  • websites operated by Mendai; and
  • any related software, APIs, and digital services (collectively, the “Service”).

By creating an account or using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Service.

2. Our Commitment to Privacy

Mendai is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

We strive to:

  • collect only information reasonably necessary to operate and improve the Service;
  • provide users with meaningful privacy choices where available;
  • use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information;
  • comply with applicable privacy laws.

3. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected:

  • through the Mendai application;
  • through our websites;
  • through AI Guide interactions;
  • through customer support communications;
  • during account registration;
  • during use of AI matching features;
  • through surveys, feedback, and promotions operated by Mendai.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to information collected by third-party websites or services that are governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Mendai, we may collect different categories of information. These categories may include:

  • personal information; account information; profile information;
  • device information; technical information; communications;
  • AI interactions; usage information; payment-related information;
  • safety and moderation information.

5. Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information including:

  • your name, username, email address, password, profile photograph, biography;
  • age or date of birth (where applicable), gender identity (if provided), pronouns (if provided);
  • interests, hobbies, goals, languages, relationship preferences;
  • location you choose to share, profile descriptions, social links you choose to include;
  • uploaded images, videos, audio, messages, comments, reactions, feedback, customer support communications.

You may choose not to provide certain information; however, doing so may limit your ability to use certain features.

6. AI Guide Conversations

If you communicate with the AI Guide, we may collect information contained within those conversations. This may include information that you voluntarily share, such as:

  • interests, goals, hobbies, preferences, life experiences;
  • communication style, requested topics, feedback, and other provided details.
Notice on AI Sharing:

We encourage users not to share information they are uncomfortable sharing with any online service.

7. AI Memory

If AI memory features are available, the AI Guide may retain certain information that you voluntarily provide in order to personalize future conversations, improve continuity, remember preferences, provide contextually relevant responses, and improve matching recommendations.

Where available, Mendai may provide tools allowing users to review, edit, or delete AI memory associated with their account.

8. AI Matching Information

To generate compatibility recommendations, Mendai may process information including profile details, selected interests, communication preferences, optional questionnaires, interactions within the Service, engagement with AI Guide, community participation, and other information described in this Privacy Policy.

AI-generated recommendations are based on automated systems and may not always be accurate or complete.

9. Information Collected Automatically

When you use Mendai, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including device model, operating system, application version, language settings, device identifiers where permitted by law, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, crash reports, performance diagnostics, network information, log data, and security events.

10. Usage Information

We may collect information about how you use Mendai, including features accessed, pages viewed, screens visited, buttons selected, searches performed, AI conversations initiated, session duration, interactions with recommendations, content engagement, and account settings.

11. Location Information

Depending on your device settings and permissions, Mendai may collect location information, which may include approximate location, city, state or province, country, and device-provided location if enabled. Certain features may be unavailable without location access.

12. Contacts

If you choose to grant permission, Mendai may access your device contacts solely for features that require such access, such as helping you connect with people you already know. Granting access is optional.

13. Camera, Photos, and Microphone

If you grant permission, Mendai may access your camera, photo library, or microphone. These permissions are used only to support features you choose to use, such as profile photos, uploading videos, voice messages, AI voice conversations, and video messages.

14. Payment Information

If you purchase subscriptions or other paid features, payment information is generally processed by third-party payment processors such as Apple App Store or Google Play. Mendai generally does not receive your complete payment card number.

15. Information from Third Parties

We may receive limited information from third parties, including app stores, authentication providers, analytics providers, fraud prevention providers, security providers, and customer support providers.

16. Information Related to Safety

To promote user safety, we may collect and maintain records related to user reports, moderation actions, blocked accounts, account warnings, appeals, investigations, abuse prevention, fraud prevention, and security incidents.

17. Sensitive Information

Some users may voluntarily choose to share information that could be considered sensitive under applicable law (e.g., discussions of emotional well-being, life experiences, personal challenges, religious beliefs, political opinions, sexual orientation, racial background, health-related topics).

Mendai requests that users exercise caution before sharing sensitive information.

18. Information You Should Not Share

Security Advisory:

For your own protection, we strongly discourage sharing Social Security numbers, driver’s license numbers, passport numbers, banking credentials, credit card numbers in chat, account passwords, security question answers, cryptocurrency wallet recovery phrases, or confidential business information.
